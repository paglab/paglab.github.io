The March 2026 winter hackathon "AI Meets Drones" will challenge students to apply artificial intelligence and remote sensing techniques to real UAV-borne imagery. Participants will work with multispectral data collected using advanced drone-based sensors.

Over an intensive two-week period, student teams will perform data processing, extract meaningful features, compute vegetation indices, and train machine learning models for agricultural applications.
The hackathon will conclude with student presentations and reports showcasing their innovative solutions.
