🌾 **Successful Conclusion of the TUM–NAU Smart Agriculture Seminar 2025** 🌾

On **June 27, 2025**, we successfully hosted the **TUM–NAU Smart Agriculture Seminar**, an international event jointly organized by the **Technical University of Munich (TUM)** and **Nanjing Agricultural University (NAU)**.  

The seminar focused on the theme:

### *Remote Sensing and Data-Driven Methods for Smart Agriculture* 🚁🛰️

Held in-person at the TUM Dürnast Research Station, the seminar brought together researchers, students, and professionals to discuss cutting-edge developments including:

- UAV- and satellite-based monitoring of crop traits  
- Machine learning for agricultural decision-making  
- Modeling plant-soil interactions for precision farming  

Throughout the day, speakers shared exciting research on topics such as **SIF retrieval from aerial imaging sensors, evapotranspiration modeling, multi-source data fusion for maize lodging prediction, and advanced approaches to nitrogen and water management**.  

The lively presentations were followed by a stimulating discussion session — and we wrapped up the day with a relaxed BBQ and beer 🍻, fostering informal exchange and future collaborations.

A big thank you to all participants and special thanks to the guests from NAU and Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences for making this event possible.
