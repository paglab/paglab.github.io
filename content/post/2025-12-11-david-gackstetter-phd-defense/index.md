We are delighted to announce the successful doctoral defense of **David Gackstetter**, jointly supervised by Prof. Marco Körner (TUM Chair of Remote Sensing Technology) and Prof. Kang Yu (TUM Precision Agriculture Lab). David has completed his dissertation titled:

**"Spatial Generalization in Satellite Image Time Series Classification through Transfer Learning and Data Harmonization."**

David's research addresses one of the central challenges in large-scale agricultural land use monitoring and remote sensing: ensuring that machine learning models trained on satellite image time series remain robust, transferable, and spatially generalizable across diverse landscapes. His work advances the scientific understanding of transfer learning, domain adaptation, and harmonized multi-sensor satellite data, contributing valuable methodological innovations to the remote sensing and digital agriculture community.
